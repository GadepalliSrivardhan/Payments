7.1 STEP2 — Pan European Automated Clearing House

What is STEP2-T Platform?

STEP2 is the pan-European Automated Clearing House (ACH) operated by EBA CLEARING. It processes SEPA Credit Transfers (SCT), SEPA Direct Debits (SDD), and supports ancillary settlement. The STEP2-T platform is the current iteration — a highly resilient, scalable infrastructure processing billions of transactions annually across 36 SEPA countries. STEP2 connects approximately 2,500+ payment service providers across Europe through a single multilateral platform.

Phases in Payment Transaction Processing

1

Submission

PSPs submit payment files to STEP2 during defined submission windows. Files are in ISO 20022 XML format (pacs.008 for SCT, pacs.003 for SDD).

2

Validation

STEP2 validates each transaction: format checks, BIC/IBAN validation, scheme rule compliance. Invalid transactions are rejected with reason codes.

3

Clearing

Valid transactions are sorted by receiving PSP. Bilateral net positions are calculated between all participant pairs.

4

Settlement

Net settlement positions are passed to T2 (via ancillary system interface). T2 settles the net amounts in central bank money across participant MCAs.

5

Delivery

After settlement, STEP2 delivers individual payment details to each receiving PSP via their STEP2 output files.

Processing Modes and Continuous Gross Settlement Model

STEP2 offers multiple processing modes: standard batch cycles (multiple times per day for SCT), bulk processing (large file submissions), and a Continuous Gross Settlement (CGS) model for SEPA Instant. In CGS mode, each instant transaction is validated and submitted for gross real-time settlement immediately rather than being batched.

7.2 STEP2 Credit Transfer (SCT) System

SCT Overview

The SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) scheme is a push payment — the payer's bank initiates the transfer. Key features: euro-only, IBAN mandatory (BIC optional since 2016), no upper limit set by scheme (though banks may impose limits), maximum execution time D+1 (next business day), and uses ISO 20022 XML messaging (pacs.008). The SCT scheme rules are defined in the EPC SCT Rulebook, updated annually.

SCT Settlement Model

SCT settlement occurs via STEP2's ancillary system connection to T2. Net positions calculated by STEP2 are submitted to T2 for settlement during defined settlement cycles (multiple times per business day). Settlement is in central bank money via participant MCAs. D+1 means a payment submitted on Monday settles by end of Tuesday.

7.3 STEP2 Direct Debit (SDD) System

SDD Overview — Core and B2B Schemes

The SEPA Direct Debit (SDD) is a pull payment — the payee's PSP initiates the collection after receiving a mandate from the payer. Two schemes exist: SDD Core (for consumer debtors — individuals) and SDD B2B (for business debtors). SDD Core allows debtors to request a refund up to 8 weeks after debit (13 months if no valid mandate). SDD B2B has no refund right and the debtor bank must check mandate existence before executing.

SDD Settlement Model

SDD pre-settlement timelines are critical: SDD Core requires advance presentation (D-1 for first/one-off, D-1 for recurrent since 2013 update) before settlement on D. SDD B2B requires D-1 advance presentation. Creditor PSPs must provide the mandate reference in all collection files. STEP2 validates mandate consistency checks where applicable.

7.4 SEPA Instant Credit Transfer Scheme (SCT Inst) and TIPS

What is SCT Inst?

The SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst) scheme, governed by the EPC, enables real-time euro payments 24/7/365 across SEPA. Key rules: maximum execution time 10 seconds end-to-end (25 seconds in exception); scheme default amount limit €100,000 per instruction; uses ISO 20022 XML messaging; available every day of the year with no downtime. From 9 January 2025, SCT Inst became mandatory for EU banks that already offer SCT to customers (for inbound payments). From 9 October 2025, mandatory for outbound as well.

TIPS vs RT1 — CSM Options for SCT Inst

TIPS (ECB) — TARGET Instant Payment Settlement. Operated by the ECB/Eurosystem. Settles each SCT Inst transaction gross in central bank money (TIPS-DCA) in real time. Available 24/7/365. Part of the T2 consolidated platform. True RTGS model for instant payments.
RT1 (EBA CLEARING) — Real-Time 1. Operated by EBA CLEARING. Real-time gross settlement system for SCT Inst. Processes transactions in under 1 second on average. Also 24/7/365. Provides an alternative to TIPS for PSPs that already have STEP2 connectivity.

7.5 SCT Inst Workflow — 7 Steps

1

Originator → Originator PSP (pain.001)

Originator sends SCT Inst instruction to their PSP (bank). Originator PSP executes validation checks and funds availability. If successful, reserves the amount on the originator's account and timestamps the transaction (this is the start time for the 10-second clock). Prepares pacs.008 SCT Inst Transaction.

2

Originator PSP → CSM (pacs.008)

Originator PSP sends pacs.008 SCT Inst Transaction to the CSM (TIPS or RT1). Simultaneously authorises the CSM to reserve funds on its settlement account as cover. This provides upfront settlement certainty to the CSM.

3

CSM → Beneficiary PSP (pacs.008)

CSM sends the pacs.008 to the Beneficiary PSP. Receipt of this message gives the Beneficiary PSP settlement certainty — it knows it will receive the funds. Beneficiary PSP verifies it can apply the transaction and executes validation checks.

4

Beneficiary PSP → CSM (pacs.002 Accept/Reject)

Beneficiary PSP sends pacs.002 to CSM indicating positive acceptance (ACCP) or rejection (RJCT). CSM acknowledges receipt. On acceptance, CSM executes settlement: debits Originator PSP's account, credits Beneficiary PSP's account.

5

Beneficiary PSP Credits Customer

Beneficiary PSP immediately makes funds available to the beneficiary customer. Funds must be made available instantly upon receipt of the positive CSM settlement confirmation.

6

CSM → Originator PSP (pacs.002 Final Confirmation)

CSM sends final confirmation to Originator PSP confirming whether the SCT Inst transaction was successful or not. Settlement is completed.

7

Originator PSP → Originator (pain.002)

If transaction successful: reservation is converted to actual debit; originator is notified via payment status report. If unsuccessful: reservation is released immediately, originator is notified of failure with reason code.

7.7 PAIN, PACS and CAMT — Message Space Explained

📤

PAIN — PAyment INitiation

Used between customer and bank (C2B space). Customers send payment instructions (pain.001) and receive status reports (pain.002). Used exclusively in the customer-to-bank space. Not used in interbank messaging.

🔄

PACS — PAyment Clearing & Settlement

Used between financial institutions (interbank space). Transfers funds between banks. pacs.008 for credit transfers, pacs.003 for direct debits, pacs.004 for returns. Used exclusively interbank.

💼

CAMT — CAsh ManagemenT

Used in both C2B and interbank. In C2B: informs customer of account activity (camt.052/053/054). Both spaces: cancellation requests (camt.055/056) and investigation resolution (camt.029).
