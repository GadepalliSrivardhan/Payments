6.1 Introduction to SEPA

SEPA (Single Euro Payments Area) was created to harmonise non-cash euro payments across Europe, making cross-border euro payments as easy and cheap as domestic payments. SEPA covers 36 countries — all 27 EU member states plus Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland, United Kingdom, Monaco, San Marino, Andorra, Vatican City, and others. SEPA payments are processed using standardised rules defined by the European Payments Council (EPC).

SEPA Payment Schemes

  • SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) — effective January 2008. Standard credit transfer, max D+1 settlement.
  • SEPA Direct Debit Core (SDD Core) — effective November 2009. Consumer direct debits with mandate.
  • SEPA Direct Debit B2B (SDD B2B) — effective November 2009. Business-to-business direct debits.
  • SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst) — effective November 2017. Real-time, 10-second execution, 24/7/365.
  • SEPA Request to Pay (SRtp) — introduced June 2021. Digital request-to-pay message preceding a payment.

6.2 Structure of T2/T2S Consolidated Platform

The TARGET Consolidation project (launched 20 March 2023) merged three separate Eurosystem market infrastructures onto a single technical platform: T2 (formerly TARGET2, the RTGS system), T2S (TARGET2-Securities, the securities settlement platform), and TIPS (TARGET Instant Payment Settlement) — all accessible through a single gateway: ESMIG.

This consolidation significantly simplifies connectivity for banks (one gateway instead of three), enables more efficient cross-system liquidity management, and standardises all messaging on ISO 20022.

🏛️

T2 (RTGS)

Real-Time Gross Settlement for large-value euro payments. Replaces TARGET2. Uses ISO 20022 exclusively. Settles ~€2.2 trillion daily.

📈

T2S (Securities)

Pan-European securities settlement platform. Settles securities transactions DvP (Delivery vs Payment) in central bank money.

⚡

TIPS (Instant)

TARGET Instant Payment Settlement — real-time gross settlement of SEPA Instant (SCT Inst) payments, 24/7/365, in central bank money.

6.4 TARGET Consolidation, ESMIG and CLM

ESMIG — Eurosystem Single Market Infrastructure Gateway

ESMIG is the single access point for all Eurosystem market infrastructures (T2, T2S, TIPS, ECMS). Before consolidation, banks needed separate connectivity to TARGET2, T2S, and TIPS. With ESMIG, one connection provides access to all services. ESMIG handles authentication, message routing, and network access management. It supports both the SWIFT network and internet-based access (A2A — application-to-application).

CLM — Central Liquidity Management

CLM (Central Liquidity Management) is a new component introduced with the T2 consolidation. It is the central hub for managing liquidity across all Eurosystem services. CLM functions include: managing minimum reserve requirements and reserve maintenance periods, processing liquidity credit transfers between MCAs and DCAs, processing intraday credit (against pledged collateral), automated liquidity transfers (auto-collateralisation in T2S), and providing liquidity monitoring and alerts to participants.

6.6 T2 Payment Transaction Types, Features and Services

Payment Transaction Types

  • Customer Credit Transfers — payments initiated by bank customers, processed through T2 for same-day settlement (pacs.008)
  • Interbank / FI-to-FI Transfers — treasury and money market transactions between banks (pacs.009)
  • Ancillary System Settlement — retail clearing systems (STEP2, CORE, Equens etc.) submit net settlement files; T2 settles them via technical accounts
  • Liquidity Transfers — automated or manual transfers between MCA, TIPS-DCA, and T2S-DCA

T2 Features and Services

  • Queue Management: Payments queued when insufficient funds; prioritised FIFO or by value
  • Gridlock Resolution: Algorithm resolves circular payment dependencies
  • Throughput Guidelines: ECB recommends % of daily value to be settled by specific times
  • Liquidity Reservation: Participants can reserve liquidity for specific payment types (e.g., cash withdrawals)
  • Intraday Credit: Provided by NCBs against eligible collateral, interest-free if repaid same day

6.7 T2 Liquidity Transfers for RTGS, TIPS and T2S

A key benefit of the consolidated T2 platform is seamless liquidity movement between the RTGS (MCA), TIPS (DCA), and T2S (DCA) components.

1

MCA → TIPS-DCA

Bank sweeps funds from its Main Cash Account to its TIPS Dedicated Cash Account to fund instant payment settlement. Can be automated (floor/ceiling rules) or manual.

2

MCA → T2S-DCA

Bank moves funds to T2S-DCA for securities settlement. Auto-collateralisation allows T2S to automatically transfer securities as collateral and credit the DCA for urgent settlement needs.

3

End-of-Day Sweeping

At end of day, all TIPS-DCA and T2S-DCA balances can be automatically swept back to the MCA. This consolidates liquidity and ensures minimum reserve requirements are met at the MCA level.
